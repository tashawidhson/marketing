How We Decide

Eight Tenets

Our job in FY27 is not to do more marketing — it's to run fewer, better programs against Microsoft and Salesforce.

1
Leverage before labor. Prioritize what pays pipeline dividends without proportional headcount.
Why it matters: a three-person team can't out-labor a bigger competitor — it has to out-leverage them with Marketplace, MACC, specializations, and evergreen retargeting.
2
Ecosystem before open market. Every campaign speaks first to the Microsoft/Salesforce base.
Why it matters: intent and budget already exist inside the ecosystem — marketing to the open market competes for attention that hasn't been pre-qualified.
3
Cadence before campaigns. One reliable monthly webinar/podcast beats a bigger one-off.
Why it matters: a consistent monthly rhythm compounds — one strong campaign that doesn't repeat produces a spike, not a pipeline.
4
Commercial result, not volume. Every campaign needs named accounts, a sponsor, an owner, and a pipeline goal.
Why it matters: events without a speaking-slot plan produce visibility, not pipeline — every program needs a stated commercial goal before it goes on the calendar.
5
One seller model. Every relationship maps to Sales's Key/Target/Opportunistic tiers.
Why it matters: a second, marketing-only tiering system would fragment seller attention instead of concentrating it on the accounts Sales already prioritizes.
6
Branded decks only, everywhere.
Why it matters: branded campaigns return roughly 19x more per dollar than generic ones — consistency is a performance lever, not just a style rule.
7
Expert, not salesy.
Why it matters: thought leadership shapes credibility with technical buyers — a pitch-toned message undercuts the expert positioning the ecosystem responds to.
