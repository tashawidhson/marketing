How We Decide

Eight Tenets

Our job in FY27 is not to do more marketing — it's to run fewer, better programs against Microsoft and Salesforce.

1
Leverage before labor. Programs that pay pipeline dividends without proportional headcount come first.
2
Ecosystem before open market. Every campaign speaks first to the Microsoft/Salesforce install base.
3
Cadence before campaigns. One reliable monthly webinar and podcast beats a bigger one-off effort.
4
Three people, one motion. Every program needs a single owner; we run no more than we can sustain.
5
Expert, not salesy. One consistent message across every channel and contributor.
6
Branded decks only. Every client meeting and Digital Sales Room. No exceptions.
7
Commercial result, not content volume. Judged by named accounts, seller sponsor, pipeline goal.
8
One seller model, not two. Every seller relationship maps to Sales's shared tiers.
